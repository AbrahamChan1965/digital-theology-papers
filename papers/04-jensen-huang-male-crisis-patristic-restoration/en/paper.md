# Integrative Inquiry into the Crisis and Holistic Restoration of Asian Male Elites Aged 40–70: Neuroscience, Exercise Science, Meta-Psychology, and Patristic Spiritual Direction

**Abstract:** Following the November 2026 resignation of 63-year-old Nvidia co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang amid multiple extramarital affair revelations and national security inquiries, this paper constructs an integrative framework combining neuroscience, exercise science, meta-psychology, and patristic spiritual direction. It examines prefrontal white-matter myelin decline, HPT/HPA dual-axis dysregulation, and early-life epigenetic sensitization in men aged 40–70; analyzes the cross-cultural "double mask" and assimilation narrative; and moves beyond post-Freudian therapeutic pastoral theology to recover the Desert Fathers' diagnostic of the eight *logismoi* and the classical path of *nepsis*, *exagoreusis*, *penthos*, and *askesis*.
